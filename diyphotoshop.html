Diy Photoshop

This diy photoshop was created using p5js. For the aesthetic of the brushes, I wanted to have a pink monochromatic color with 4 different kinds of brushes and one eraser brush. With these brushes I drew a picture of my cat, Mocha.

Press: 1-Flower brush, 2-Thin brush, 3-Airbrush, 4-Thick brush, 5-Eraser
